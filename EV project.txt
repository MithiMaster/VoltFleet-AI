Project Statement:
Build a comprehensive application for electric vehicle (EV) fleet management to help fleet managers
efficiently monitor and manage their EVs. The system will track real-time vehicle performance,
battery health, charging status, and driver behavior. It will also provide route optimization, vehicle
maintenance alerts, and cost analysis based on energy consumption. The application will include
user authentication and report generation features.
Outcomes
The Real-Time EV Fleet Monitoring and Predictive Analytics Solution will enable fleet managers to
monitor their electric vehicles' performance and health in real-time. This includes tracking battery
status, charging levels and optimizing fleet operations. This reduces operational costs and ensures
vehicles are maintained proactively, enhancing overall fleet efficiency. The system will offer
comprehensive cost and energy consumption analysis, along with customizable report generation.
Modules to be Implemented:

Implement user registration and login with email verification.

Define roles for fleet managers and drivers.

Allow registration of EVs with details such as make, model, battery capacity, and service history.

Monitor real-time data including location, speed, battery status, and charging status.

Implement monitoring to alert fleet managers when battery health degrades or requires charging.

Analyze driver behavior (e.g., speed, braking, acceleration) and its impact on energy consumption.
