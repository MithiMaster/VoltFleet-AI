# 20 Curated Open-Access Research Papers (2020–2026)

This collection contains **20 high-impact, open-access (free full-text / non-purchasing)** research papers published between **2020 and 2026** directly related to the **VoltGrid EV Fleet Management & Predictive Analytics** project.

### Research Pillars Covered:
1. **EV Range Prediction & Energy Consumption Modeling (ML)**
2. **Driver Behavior, Driving Style & Eco-Driving Performance Scoring**
3. **EV Fleet Management, Smart Routing & Charging Scheduling**
4. **Battery Health (SoH/SoC) Monitoring & Telematics**
